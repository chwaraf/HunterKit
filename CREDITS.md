## Sound assets (`Media/*.ogg`)

The four bundled blaster "pew" files are **original synthesised sound effects**,
generated by the addon's author from a downward frequency chirp (sine sweep) with
a noise bite and an exponential decay tail. No third-party audio was used.

### Bring-your-own pews

Users may drop their own `pew-N.ogg` files into `Media/` to replace the bundled
ones, provided the replacement audio is licensed for redistribution (CC0, CC-BY,
or public domain). If you ship a CC-BY file, keep its attribution here and in the
README.
